Product safety summary prepared by Maple Leaf Solvents. Rev. 5.6 (2024).

This document covers Dimethyl sulfoxide (CAS 67-68-5). Under GHS the product is classified as Not classified, and no signal word is required. In case of fire: use dry chemical, CO2 or alcohol-resistant foam.

Physical data: the substance boils at 189 °C. Its closed-cup flash point is 87 °C.
