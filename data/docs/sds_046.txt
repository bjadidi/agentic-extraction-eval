Product safety summary prepared by Maple Leaf Solvents. Rev. 1.5 (2024).

This document covers Mineral oil (CAS 8042-47-5). Under GHS the product is classified as Aspiration hazard, Category 1, and the signal word is 'Danger'. Avoid release to the environment. Dispose of contents in accordance with local regulations.

Physical data: the substance boils at 572.0 °F. Its closed-cup flash point is 365.0 °F.
