Product safety summary prepared by Harbor Industrial. Rev. 6.4 (2023).

This document covers Sodium hydroxide solution 50% (CAS 1310-73-2). Under GHS the product is classified as Skin corrosion, Category 1A, and the signal word is 'Danger'. Avoid release to the environment. Dispose of contents in accordance with local regulations.

Physical data: the substance boils at 284.0 °F. A flash point is not applicable. The pH of the supplied product is approximately 14.0.
