Product safety summary prepared by Northwind Chemicals. Rev. 3.4 (2025).

This document covers Propylene glycol (CAS 57-55-6). Under GHS the product is classified as Not classified, and no signal word is required. Wear protective gloves, eye protection and face protection.

Physical data: the substance boils at 188 °C. Its closed-cup flash point is 99 °C.
