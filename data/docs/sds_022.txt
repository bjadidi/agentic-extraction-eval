Product safety summary prepared by Granite Fine Chemicals. Rev. 8.6 (2019).

This document covers Citric acid solution 10% (CAS 77-92-9). Under GHS the product is classified as Eye irritation, Category 2, and the signal word is 'Warning'. Wear protective gloves, eye protection and face protection.

Physical data: the substance boils at 100 °C. A flash point is not applicable. The pH of the supplied product is approximately 2.2.
