Product safety summary prepared by Granite Fine Chemicals. Rev. 2.8 (2022).

This document covers Hydrogen peroxide 30% (CAS 7722-84-1). Under GHS the product is classified as Oxidizing liquid, Category 2, and the signal word is 'Danger'. Keep container tightly closed. Store in a cool, well-ventilated place.

Physical data: the substance boils at 222.8 °F. A flash point is not applicable. The pH of the supplied product is approximately 3.5.
