Product safety summary prepared by Lakeside Reagents. Rev. 3.6 (2019).

This document covers n-Butanol (CAS 71-36-3). Under GHS the product is classified as Flammable liquid, Category 3, and the signal word is 'Warning'. Avoid release to the environment. Dispose of contents in accordance with local regulations.

Physical data: the substance boils at 118 °C. Its closed-cup flash point is 35 °C.
