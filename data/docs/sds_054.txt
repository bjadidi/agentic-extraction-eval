Product safety summary prepared by Lakeside Reagents. Rev. 9.6 (2021).

This document covers Glycerol (CAS 56-81-5). Under GHS the product is classified as Not classified, and no signal word is required. Avoid release to the environment. Dispose of contents in accordance with local regulations.

Physical data: the substance boils at 290 °C. Its closed-cup flash point is 160 °C.
