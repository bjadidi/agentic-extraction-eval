Product safety summary prepared by Polaris Lab Supply. Rev. 9.8 (2025).

This document covers Ethyl acetate (CAS 141-78-6). Under GHS the product is classified as Flammable liquid, Category 2, and the signal word is 'Danger'. In case of fire: use dry chemical, CO2 or alcohol-resistant foam.

Physical data: the substance boils at 77 °C. Its closed-cup flash point is -4 °C.
