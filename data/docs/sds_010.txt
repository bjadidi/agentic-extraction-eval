Product safety summary prepared by Harbor Industrial. Rev. 6.2 (2022).

This document covers Toluene (CAS 108-88-3). Under GHS the product is classified as Flammable liquid, Category 2, and the signal word is 'Danger'. Avoid release to the environment. Dispose of contents in accordance with local regulations.

Physical data: the substance boils at 111 °C. Its closed-cup flash point is 4 °C.
