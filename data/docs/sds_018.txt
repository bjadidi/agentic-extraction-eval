Product safety summary prepared by Polaris Lab Supply. Rev. 6.6 (2020).

This document covers n-Butanol (CAS 71-36-3). Under GHS the product is classified as Flammable liquid, Category 3, and the signal word is 'Warning'. Wear protective gloves, eye protection and face protection.

Physical data: the substance boils at 118 °C. Its closed-cup flash point is 35 °C.
